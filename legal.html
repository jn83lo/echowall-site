What EchoWall does

EchoWall lets a business collect testimonials from its customers via a simple form, review and approve them, and display approved testimonials on its own website using an embeddable widget.

Accounts and data

When you sign up, we store your email, your business name, and a URL slug you choose. Testimonials submitted through your form (name, role, quote, and rating) are stored and associated with your account. Only approved testimonials are ever shown publicly through the widget.

You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through PayPal; your account will revert to the free plan once PayPal confirms the cancellation.
