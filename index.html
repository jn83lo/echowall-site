The problem

Great feedback lives in email and DMs. It never makes it to your site.

Every small business owner has praise sitting in their inbox. Turning it into a testimonials page usually means designing something, copy-pasting quotes, and asking permission — so it never happens.

  1. 01

    Send one link

    After a project or purchase, send your customer a short form. Two questions, a star rating, optional photo.

  2. 02

    Approve what comes in

    Every submission lands in your dashboard first. You choose what goes live — nothing posts automatically.

  3. 03

    Paste one snippet

    Drop a single line of code into your site. The wall updates itself whenever you approve something new.
